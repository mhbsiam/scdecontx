# Soup scenarios

Generated by `scripts/scenarios.py` from `tests/_soup.py`. Mean over 3 seeds. *Total* is all soup counts; *foreign* is the soup counts from other clusters, the part the model can see. *Worst cluster bias* is the mean error of the cluster with the largest one. *Counts relative error* is ‖N̂ − N‖_F / ‖N‖_F against the true native counts N. *Marker leak* is the share of contaminating marker counts left in the corrected counts; *over-removal* the share of native counts removed.

*oracle* is the posterior under the true parameters, the floor that sampling noise leaves to any method. Marker leak and over-removal count only one sign of the error, so even the oracle has nonzero values. Soup of the cell's own type lies on its native genes, so removing it raises over-removal: read over-removal against the oracle, not as lower is better. Counts relative error weighs both kinds of error.
